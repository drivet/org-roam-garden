#+title: The Complete Guide to Atomic Note Taking

Describes an iceberg with 4 levels of taking notes:

 * Just take notes, link them together
 * Take atomic notes, with heruistics, like having an easy title, or a
   "short" note.
 * Understand that the heuristics are part of a bigger system of knowledge,
   where we truly understand what an atomic note is, as an element of
   thinking.
 * Level 4 is weird, it's basically just understanding that level 3 exists

Steps to create an atomic note:

 * Just make the note.  Don't worry about it.
 * Edit it.  Remove unnecessary stuff.  Is it one idea?  Do you have t9o
   break it up?  Is the title good?  Do you have a good one line summary?
 * Identify the knowledge block.

Tiers of notes and ideas.

| Tier | The Idea | What to do with it |
----------------------------------------
| S | Major leap for your thinking, very likely to support a current and important project                      | Unleash the kraken                                                  |
| A | Improves your thinking, supports a current project, helps with future projects or areas of responsibility | Get at least to level 2, consider level 3 and 4                     |
| B | Seems useful,  a clear connection to an area of responsibility                                            | go to level 2                                                       |
| C | Interesting, possible connection to an area of responsibility                                             | Don’t sweat it, consider putting in your Rumen                      |
| D | Interesting, has some connection to your life                                                             | Put it somewhere, but not in your Zettelkasten, possibly your Rumen |
| F | Interesting                                                                                               | Nothin g                                                            |
