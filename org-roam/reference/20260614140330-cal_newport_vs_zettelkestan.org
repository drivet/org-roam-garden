Zettelkestan guru, processing a book isn't the same as taking notes.  You're
basically integrating the knowledge gained from the book into the knowledge
that already exists in your Zettelkestan.

The reference material doesn't necessarily exist as a discrete thing anymore,
unattached to anything else.
