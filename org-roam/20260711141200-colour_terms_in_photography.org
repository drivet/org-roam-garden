** White Balance

This is about making sure your photos appear as they do in real life.
Cameras capture colour casts or tints that eyes tend to filter out.

Adjusting colour along two spectrums:

 * blue-yellow  (natural light, colour temperature)
 * green-magenta (some artificial light)
